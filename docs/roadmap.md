# Public readiness path

Tandem follows evidence, not calendar pressure. The protocol is finalized. Mainnet remains inactive until every required safety and verification outcome is complete and an explicit launch decision is recorded.

This public roadmap describes outcomes users can verify. It does not publish internal schedules, owners, infrastructure, or operational procedures.

## Finalized

- Authoritative normative protocol bytes
- Public specification hash and byte contract
- Strict public manifest and agreement schemas
- Deterministic golden vectors
- Public product explanation and repository boundary

## Evidence required before activation

- Independent reproduction and signing of the normative artifact
- Complete valid, invalid, precedence, root, and reorganization corpus
- Agreement between independent parsers and indexers
- Continuous public test-network agreement over an approved observation period
- Complete recovery creation, export, restore, maturity, fee, and reorganization drills
- Verified signer behavior without blind signing
- Safe ordinary coin selection around known and unknown protected outputs
- Independent protocol, wallet, web, content, infrastructure, and dependency security reviews
- Accessible product flows across keyboard, screen reader, contrast, motion, and zoom needs
- Production rehearsal, rollback, monitoring, backup, and incident readiness
- Explicit signed activation authority after every preceding outcome is green

## Activation principles

- Missing evidence is a failed gate.
- A deployed website or service is not activation.
- A configured transaction or funded address is not activation.
- Mainnet construction and broadcast remain unavailable until authorization.
- Indexer disagreement blocks official state-dependent construction and broadcast.

## After activation

Public status should identify the selected network binding, verification state, supported signer paths, known limitations, and incident communication channel. These claims must be backed by current evidence, not marketing language.
